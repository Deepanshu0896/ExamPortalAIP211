Official Examination Guidelines & Candidate SOP

Candidate Standard Operating Procedure, Marking Scheme, Navigation Walkthrough, and IIIT Kota B.Tech Curriculum Matrix.

1. Extended Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

All examinees must strictly comply with the following institutional procedures across all phases of the evaluation:

A. Pre-Examination Setup & Authentication

  1. Mandatory Authentication: Under IIIT Kota Option 2 Protected Mode, examinees must sign in with their authorized student Roll Number (e.g., 21CS101) and password prior to launching an examination session.
  2. Hardware & Network Readiness: Ensure a stable computer setup and reliable broadband connection. System requirements are detailed below:
IIIT Kota Evaluation Cell — Candidate System Pre-Check Checklist:
- Supported Browsers: Google Chrome (v90+), Mozilla Firefox (v88+), Microsoft Edge (v90+)
- Screen Resolution: Recommended 1280x720 or higher
- JavaScript Engine: Enabled (ECMAScript 6+ standard required for timer and DOM interaction)
- Network Stability: Continuous ping to academic server required for live session timing

B. In-Examination Conduct & Proctoring Rules

  1. Session Timing & Countdown: The examination duration is strictly 10 minutes (600 seconds). A sticky timer bar continuously displays remaining time. A pulse warning triggers when less than 02:00 minutes remain.
  2. Paper Lock Safeguard: Upcoming or sealed papers (such as CS203: Database Management Systems) are locked by the faculty administrator and cannot be accessed or attempted by students.
  3. Window Focus & Integrity: Candidates must remain in the active browser tab. Navigating away or minimizing the examination window is strictly prohibited.

C. Post-Examination & Submission Protocol

  1. Manual Submission: Clicking "Finalize & Submit Exam" triggers a confirmation modal dialogue requiring examinee verification before final transmission.
  2. Automatic Submission: Upon conclusion of the countdown timer (T0 = 00:00), the system automatically compiles and submits all currently selected responses.
  3. Scorecard Archival: Upon submission, responses are evaluated against the official answer key and recorded permanently in the university database.

2. Comprehensive Marking Scheme & 10-Point Grading Scale

Evaluations strictly follow the grading ordinance of Indian Institute of Information Technology Kota (IIIT Kota):

Evaluation Structure per Module:

Total Questions: 5 MCQs  |  Correct Answer: +2.0 Marks  |  Incorrect / Unanswered: 0.0 Marks (No Negative Marking)  |  Maximum Marks: 10.0

Academic Equivalence & SGPA Formula:

Percentage Marks = CGPA × 10  |  SGPA = ∑(Ci × Gi) ÷ ∑Ci (where Ci = Course Credits, Gi = Grade Points in Cohort Kth)

3. Portal Navigation & Candidate Walkthrough

Follow these sequential steps to navigate through the examination lifecycle smoothly:

  1. Step 1 — Authentication: Visit the Portal Login page. Enter your university Roll Number (e.g. 21CS101) and password.
  2. Step 2 — Selecting an Active Paper: From the top navigation dropdown (Select Subject ▾) or the Home dashboard, choose your scheduled module: Web Development (CS201) or Data Structures & Algorithms (CS202).
  3. Step 3 — Attempting Questions: Read each question and select your preferred answer option. The active card automatically illuminates with an .option-selected border for clear visual feedback.
  4. Step 4 — Monitoring Time: Glance at the sticky timer bar at the top of the viewport to manage your pace during the 10-minute test duration.
  5. Step 5 — Submission & Confirmation: Click the "Submit Test" button. Confirm your intent in the modal popup dialog box.
  6. Step 6 — Transcript & Scorecard: Navigate to the Scoreboard page and search with your Roll Number to inspect your verified marksheet, grade point, and SGPA.

Examination Integrity & Institutional Policies

Single-Attempt Session Policy
Each registered examinee is allocated one official evaluated submission per scheduled course module.
Institutional Transcript Auditing
All evaluated scores and grade point averages are permanently recorded in the central database under supervision of the IIIT Kota Autonomous Examination Cell.
Academic Honesty & Proctored Integrity
Attempts to tamper with client scripts, spoof credentials, or access locked papers are recorded and escalated to the Academic Disciplinary Committee.
